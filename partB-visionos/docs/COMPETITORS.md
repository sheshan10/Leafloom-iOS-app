| App | What it is known for | Business model (general) | Gap LeafLoom Spatial targets | Checked on |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## How LeafLoom Spatial differs

1. **Placement feedback, not just placement.** AR furniture apps answer "does it fit?". LeafLoom
   answers "will it *thrive* there?" by combining each plant's light preference with where it stands
   relative to the window.
2. **Your collection, life-size.** The plants are the user's own (shared with the iPhone app), not a
   catalogue.
3. **Care in context.** Watering status and tips appear on the plant in the room, so a walk around
   the room is also a care check.
4. **Private by design.** No account, no camera frames read by the app, data stored on device.

## What we learned from competitors

- Reminders and identification are table stakes on phone; LeafLoom's iPhone app already covers them.
- Light is the hardest thing for users to judge. Planta's light meter shows demand for it, which
  supports making light the centre of the spatial experience.
- Freemium with a subscription is the common model; a one-off premium unlock is less common and
  could be a differentiator (see `PITCH.md`).
